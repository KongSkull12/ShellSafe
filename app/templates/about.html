About ShellSafe

ShellSafe is an IoT-Based Real-Time Algal Bloom Detection and Predictive Analytics System designed to monitor coastal water quality and identify environmental conditions associated with harmful algal blooms. By integrating smart sensors, real-time monitoring, predictive analytics, and automated alerts, ShellSafe helps authorities make informed decisions for coastal water management and environmental protection.
