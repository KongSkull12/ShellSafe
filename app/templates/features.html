Key Features

"Advanced monitoring and predictive analytics for coastal water safety."

Real-Time Water Monitoring

Continuously monitors water temperature, pH, dissolved oxygen, salinity, and turbidity in real time.

IoT-Enabled Data Collection

Collects and transmits environmental data from coastal monitoring devices using cellular communication technology.

Predictive Analytics

Analyzes environmental conditions and predicts potential harmful algal bloom risks using intelligent analytics.

Smart Alert System

Automatically sends web notifications and SMS alerts to authorities when abnormal water conditions are detected.

Historical Data & Analytics

Provides trend analysis, charts, and historical monitoring records for informed decision-making.

Device Health Monitoring

Tracks battery level, connectivity status, and device performance to ensure reliable operation.
