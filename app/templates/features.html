What ShellSafe does

Built around the T-Call A7670 node — monitoring, alerts, and history in one place.

Live water quality

Temperature, pH, turbidity, ORP, and EC stream to Firebase and appear on the dashboard within seconds.

Honest disconnect state

Unplugged or unstable sensors display Disconnected — no zeroed “fault” readings that hide hardware issues.

On-device SMS

When thresholds trip, the A7670 modem sends SMS to recipients configured in SMS Settings (up to four numbers).

Shared risk rules

Web risk scoring matches firmware: pH 6.5–8.5, turbidity >50 NTU, temp >35°C, ORP <200 mV, EC >2000 µS/cm.

History & trends

Review charts and tables to compare conditions over time for coastal decision-making.

Device health

See online/offline device status separately from per-sensor disconnects on the Devices page.
